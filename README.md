# Up-the-River-Game
My fantastic app

# Name: Sam Elliott
The objective of the game is to make a sequence of guesses to correctly predict aspects of a drawn card. First the player will guess if the card is red or black, then they will guess if the number on the next drawn card will be higher or lower than the previous one. The next guess is if the next drawn card number will fall in between the previous two numbers or outside the range. The final guess will be the suit of the card. If any wrong guess is made, the process is restarted until all guesses are made right in sequence. 

# Intended Users 
People who are in need of some quick entertainment.
